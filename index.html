A model on your PC

The bot purchase does not include AI credits. Cloud usage follows your provider’s pricing; local Ollama requires a compatible model and hardware.

Setup, privacy and compatibility
  1. Open the bot in Windows and go to Settings → Artificial Intelligence.
  2. Configure your provider, API key and model, or connect your local Ollama instance.
  3. Enable AI and choose what to share with your bot. AI is off by default.

Windows encrypts saved API keys. AI requests can include attachments, your instructions and saved context such as conversation history or memory.

Image understanding depends on the model you select. Local AI performance depends on your hardware.
